# CrawlLogic

Production repository for Crawl Logic film concepts, script scaffolding, and loose production material.

## Business Profile

**Crawl Logic (Crawl Space Experts)** is a specialized, owner-operated home services company founded by **Josh Maynor (2019)**.  
The company delivers crawl space encapsulation, moisture remediation, drainage solutions, and structural repair with a focus on high-quality materials, educational inspections, and customer-first execution.

## Core Service Coverage (For Script/Production Use)

- **Crawl Space Encapsulation**  
  Seals crawl spaces to block moisture and pests with high-grade vapor barriers (8–125 mil).
- **Moisture & Mold Remediation**  
  Uses Aprilaire dehumidifiers and EZ Flow drainage systems to control humidity and reduce mildew risk.
- **Structural Support & Repair**  
  Performs foundational and structural wood repairs in-house.

## Differentiators to Highlight On Camera

- **Owner-operated transparency**
- **No high-pressure sales process**
- **Multiple tailored solution paths for homeowners**
- **Inspection evidence via photo/video documentation**
- **10–20 overhead crawl space lights added for visibility**
- **Professional electrician integrated into project workflow**
- **Often no deposit required; payment on completed, satisfactory work**

## Narrative Angle for Film/Script Development

Crawl Logic should be framed as a trust-first, education-first operator protecting homes from hidden damage.  
Story beats should emphasize how moisture control, structural safety, and clear homeowner communication prevent long-term property loss while improving indoor air quality.

## Script 1 — The Hidden Room

### Objective
Create urgency by revealing unseen crawl space risk, then resolve with trust and proof.

### 60s Script
**0–4s (Dark cold open, flashlight beam):**  
VO: “The most dangerous room in your house… is the one you never see.”

**5–12s (Quick cuts: damp insulation, standing water, mold spots):**  
VO: “Moisture, mold, and structural damage often start in the crawl space—quietly.”

**13–20s (On camera, owner intro):**  
On Cam: “I’m Josh with Crawl Logic. We find the problem early and fix it right.”

**21–35s (Process visuals: inspection footage, measuring humidity, showing photos):**  
VO: “We inspect, document, and walk you through options: encapsulation, drainage, dehumidification, and structural repair.”

**36–47s (Differentiators: bright lights installed, clean work area, team at work):**  
VO: “No pressure. No guesswork. Just evidence, transparency, and quality workmanship.”

**48–56s (Homeowner reaction clip or text testimonial):**  
Homeowner: “They showed us exactly what was wrong and fixed it the right way.”

**57–60s (Logo + phone + website):**  
VO: “Book your crawl space inspection with Crawl Logic today.”

### Shot List
1. Crawl entry POV with flashlight (2 takes).
2. 3–5 closeups of moisture/mold/problem areas.
3. Owner medium shot delivering intro line.
4. Tablet/phone showing real inspection photos.
5. Installing vapor barrier, drainage line, or dehumidifier.
6. Finished crawl space reveal with lights on.
7. Client reaction/interview clip.
8. End slate (logo, phone, URL).

### CTA Variants (Test 3)
- “Book your free crawl space inspection today.”
- “Call Crawl Logic now before small damage becomes a big bill.”
- “Get photo-proof of your crawl space condition this week.”

## Production Consistency Template (Use for All 5 Ads)

- **Hook (0–5s):** Pattern interrupt, problem reveal, or bold statement.
- **Problem Proof (5–15s):** Real footage of crawl conditions.
- **Authority (15–25s):** Owner on camera.
- **Solution Walkthrough (25–45s):** Visual process and service scope.
- **Trust Marker (45–55s):** Testimonial/proof/no-pressure positioning.
- **CTA (55–60s):** Clear action, contact details, and timeframe.

## CTA Testing Matrix (Simple)

For each ad, rotate:
1. **Urgency CTA** (“before next storm / before costs rise”)
2. **Transparency CTA** (“photo-documented inspection”)
3. **Outcome CTA** (“protect structure + air quality”)

Track by source code in phone number extension, QR link, or landing-page UTM.
